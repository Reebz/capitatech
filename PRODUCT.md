# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Senior decision-makers and their sponsors — CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, and board members — at firms in regulated, retention-driven industries: telco, banking, insurance, property, SaaS. They arrive cold, referred, or via outbound, weighing whether to trust a senior advisor with an AI strategy, customer-retention, pricing, or operating-model decision. Context: scanning on a laptop between meetings, deciding in under a minute whether this is a senior-operator practice or another generic consultancy. The job to be done: judge seniority, depth, and fit fast, then book a discovery call or contact directly — or leave.

## Product Purpose

A single long-scroll landing page for Capita Technology, a principal-led, independent advisory firm. It exists to convert senior buyers into discovery-call requests by signalling — through editorial craft and plain, confident copy — that this is a senior operator who delivers the work personally, not a consultancy that staffs it out to an analyst layer. Engagements are scoped and priced openly (from $35,000; bespoke on request). Success: a CFO or COO reads the hero, scans the method, and forms a "premium, considered, senior advisory" impression on the first scroll, then submits the contact form or books a call. The founder would send the URL to a peer at a CFO/COO level without caveat.

## Brand Personality

Senior, plain-spoken, and independent — carried with premium editorial confidence. The voice is direct and unhedged: short sentences, everyday words, claims named in writing ("Operators, not consultants."). It states the operating model plainly (principal-led, workshop-based, written deliverables, independent) and lets the design's restraint-plus-saturation do the premium signalling rather than adjectives. Emotional goal: a senior buyer feels respected, in capable hands, and certain that the person they see is the one who will do the work. No hype, no jargon padding, no consultancy throat-clearing.

## Anti-references

- **Generic B2B / big-four consultancy.** The conservative restraint the redesign was built to escape; reads as "staffed out," not senior-operator.
- **SaaS product landing.** Hero-metric templates, identical icon-card grids, gradient accents — the patterns that say "software company," not "advisory practice."
- **Whimsical, illustration-led coaching (humanlyagile).** The editorial layout idiom was lifted deliberately; the casual coaching whimsy and illustration tone explicitly were not.
- **AI-slop editorial.** Display-serif + italic drop caps + a tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow above every section + numbered section markers as default scaffolding — the saturated 2026 reflex that reads "which AI made this?"

## Design Principles

- **Show seniority, don't claim it.** Craft and editorial confidence carry the "premium senior advisory" message; the copy stays plain and specific. The page is itself the proof of taste.
- **Plain words, named claims.** Every assertion is concrete and falsifiable ("the principal who scopes it signs it"). No hedging, no jargon, no padding for engagement.
- **Typography is the visual weight.** Saturated plates and the display/mono type carry the load; chunky container chrome (borders, offset shadows) is reserved for cards and form fields where containment is structural. Don't decorate what type can already carry.
- **Saturation in the bookends, rest in the middle.** A Capita-blue hero and footer wordmark plus a magenta commitment band bracket a warm-gray editorial middle. This quilted band rhythm is the page's signature; protect the calm middle as deliberate rest, not as space to fill.
- **One signature, not a system of tics.** A single recurring kinetic phrase, a single marker idiom — repetition reads as art-direction theatre. Restraint is what keeps each move meaningful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 / 2.2 AA target. Body text clears 4.5:1 and large text 3:1 against its ground — the `--muted-strong` token exists specifically so small-caps mono labels pass AA on the warm-gray page ground. Full keyboard support and ARIA on the FAQ accordion (single-open, `aria-expanded`) and the country-code combobox (ARIA 1.2 contract, roving focus). Skip link and ARIA landmarks throughout. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses the ASCII spotlight grid to a static tint, freezes the kinetic-phrase travel, and reduces all transitions to near-instant. Focus-visible rings on every interactive control (SC 2.4.11), with shadow-tint swaps for perimeter coverage on chunky controls.
