# Conflicts & Resolution — Marketing Psychology vs Ogilvy

Per directive: where the two reviews disagree, Ogilvy wins.
Where they agree, both are recorded as one direction.
Where one is silent and the other recommends, the recommending side carries.

---

## Direct conflicts (Ogilvy prevails)

### 1. Hero H1 — what to lead with
- **Psych:** `Stop the retention, revenue, and AI decisions that are quietly costing you the year.` (loss aversion + jobs-to-be-done)
- **Ogilvy:** `Capita Technology now publishes packaged advisory, with prices. Bespoke continues by request.` (lead with news)
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Lead with the news of the productisation pivot. The pivot *is* the news; burying it wastes it.

### 2. CTAs
- **Psych:** Primary `See packaged engagements` / Secondary `Request a bespoke conversation`
- **Ogilvy:** Primary `See the four packages and prices.` / Secondary `Discuss a bespoke engagement.`
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Explicitly name "prices" in the primary so the reader knows what they get on click.

### 3. Number of packages
- **Psych:** Three (Diagnostic / Roadmap / Program) — centre-stage default, decoy structure.
- **Ogilvy:** Three-package or four-package version offered; four-package version is more comprehensive (adds Measurement and Governance).
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Four packages — Operating Model and Architecture, 90-Day Diagnosis, Roadmap, Measurement and Governance.

### 4. Pricing display format
- **Psych:** Bare round prices, no "from", no "starting at" — AUD 35,000 / 75,000 / 150,000.
- **Ogilvy:** "From $X" is correct when the package can run longer or wider; honest.
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Use "From $X" with the duration band.

### 5. Pricing anchoring order
- **Psych:** Lowest → middle (centre-stage default) → highest.
- **Ogilvy:** Anchor highest first (Rolls-Royce/Mercedes price-list logic).
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Operating Model and Architecture (from $85k) leads. Then Diagnosis (from $45k), Roadmap (from $35k), Measurement and Governance (from $35k).

### 6. Package names
- **Psych:** `Diagnostic` · `Roadmap` · `Program` (artifact-named).
- **Ogilvy:** `Operating Model and Architecture` · `90-Day Diagnosis` · `Roadmap` · `Measurement and Governance` (outcome-named).
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Ogilvy's names. "Operating Model Build beats Strategy Sprint."

### 7. Kinetic phrase — keep or kill
- **Psych:** Keep. Use twice. First "Operator-grade clarity." / Second "Operator-grade, not slideware."
- **Ogilvy:** Use once. Phrase should be `Operators, not consultants.` Repeating it twice is awards-bait art direction.
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Use once. Phrase: `Operators, not consultants.`

### 8. Risk-reversal mechanism
- **Psych:** "End the engagement after the diagnostic, bill only the Diagnostic." — caps perceived loss at $35k regardless of package.
- **Ogilvy:** "Our 90-day commitment" — return for a no-fee follow-up week at the 90-day mark if indicators haven't moved.
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Use the 90-day no-fee follow-up week mechanism.

### 9. Outcomes column framing
- **Psych:** Lead each column with the loss (`Retention bleed.` / `Revenue leaking out of the funnel.` / `AI programs without a defensible story.`)
- **Ogilvy:** Lead each column with the named outcome and named numbers (`Retention. Average book of retention work over forty engagements: 180–320 basis points of annual churn reduction within twelve months of plan execution. Numbers verified by the client's finance team.`)
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Lead with named outcome and specific numbers. **Requires the numbers to be true and verifiable** — see "Truth-gates" section below.

### 10. FAQ — question set
- **Psych:** Keep most existing questions, rewrite for psychology, add Q6 (price) and Q7 (success measurement).
- **Ogilvy:** Rewrite all five existing questions to procurement-grade objection-handlers, add a sixth (why you publish prices).
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Use Ogilvy's complete six-question set.

### 11. Trusted-by treatment
- **Psych:** If logos cannot be named, replace with structural text ("three of the four major Australian banks").
- **Ogilvy:** Three named, titled, photographed testimonials with the exact problem solved. If three cannot be sourced, publish two and label "Eleven additional named references available on request."
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Pursue named, titled testimonials with photographs. The structural-naming fallback is only used if zero named testimonials can be obtained.

### 12. Contact section form
- **Psych:** Keep core fields, soften textarea with placeholder examples, lower-friction submit button (`Send`).
- **Ogilvy:** Add Company and Role fields, add package-selector dropdown, replace textarea placeholder with a labelled required field, submit button `Send this to Capita`.
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Add Company, Role, package selector. Submit: `Send this to Capita`.

### 13. Contact H2
- **Psych:** `Tell us what you are trying to move. We will respond with the engagement that fits.`
- **Ogilvy:** `Tell us the number you need to move in the next 90 days. We reply within two business days with a scoped proposal and a price.`
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Specific commitments (two business days, scoped proposal, price) beat vague invitations.

### 14. Nav labels
- **Psych:** `Method / Engagements / FAQ / Contact`
- **Ogilvy:** `Packages / Bespoke / Outcomes / FAQ / Talk to us`
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Separate the Packages and Bespoke routes in nav. "Talk to us" replaces "Contact."

### 15. Founder section depth
- **Psych:** Short identity-primer section after the hero (`Capita is led by operators who have carried the same numbers you do.`)
- **Ogilvy:** Long signed founder letter between packages and FAQ — `Why we publish prices, and why we are advisory only.` Signature image.
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Long signed founder letter, positioned between packages and FAQ.

### 16. Outcomes lede
- **Psych:** `Engagements deliver written diagnostics, a 90-day roadmap, and exec-ready decision artifacts — not slideware.`
- **Ogilvy:** `Every engagement ends with a written plan, signed by the engagement lead. Below are three of the outcomes we are most often hired to move.` Strike "decision artifacts" — internal language.
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Ogilvy's lede. Strike "decision artifacts" sitewide.

### 17. Submit button
- **Psych:** `Send`
- **Ogilvy:** `Send this to Capita`
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** `Send this to Capita`

### 18. Hero eyebrow
- **Psych:** `Independent advisory · Telco · Banking · Insurance · SaaS`
- **Ogilvy:** `Independent. Operator-led. Advisory only — never the implementer.` (Keyword-soup eyebrows belong on agency portfolio sites.)
- **Resolution → Ogilvy:** Ogilvy's eyebrow.

## Agreement (both recommend; no conflict)

- **Meta description must change.** Both reject "AI-powered decision systems."
- **Method block bodies must name the specific artifact** the executive receives.
- **Outcomes need numbers / proof.** Both reject pure adjectives like "clarity."
- **FAQ must contain a price question.**
- **Risk reversal must exist** (form differs — Ogilvy wins on form).
- **Anonymous logos must be replaced or supplemented with named proof.**
- **Founder must be surfaced and named.**
- **Bespoke route must complement, not cannibalise, packages.**
- **Submit text and success message must give a specific time commitment.**
- **Footer Services/Industries tiles are SEO stuffing and must be cut.**
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks, no charm pricing, no fake scarcity.** The senior advisory register is correct and must be preserved.

## Truth-gates (must be true or do not publish)

Several Ogilvy-prevailing recommendations are claim-bearing. They are only safe to ship if the underlying fact is true and defensible.

- `Three outcomes our last forty engagements have moved` — only if the engagement count is ≥40.
- `180–320 basis points of annual churn reduction within twelve months` — only if verifiable across the retention book.
- `Advisory by ex-operators since 2019.` — only if 2019 is correct founding year.
- `Bespoke engagements start at $120,000` — must be enforced in practice.
- `Two business days` response commitment — operational SLA, must be honoured.
- Named testimonials (Sarah Lin / David Pham / Rachel Okonkwo) — placeholders. Real quotes with named permission required before publish.
- `Vendors we have no commercial relationship with` list — must be true. If any of those vendors have a referral or partner agreement, strike that vendor from the list.
- `Seven in ten engagements fit one of four shapes` — only if approximately true.
