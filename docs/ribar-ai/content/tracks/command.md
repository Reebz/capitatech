# Ribar.ai — Command track

## Site card (verbatim)

**Eyebrow:**

> $100,000 · 12 weeks · 1:1

**Name:**

> Command

**One-line promise:**

> *Strategic AI leadership. You finish with an AI strategy and governance framework you have presented — rehearsed in this program — to your actual board.*

**Inclusions:**

- 4-week shared Foundation: vocabulary, mental models, core tool fluency (adapts to your prior depth).
- 8-week Command specialization: AI architecture decisions, transformation sequencing, governance, board engagement.
- Twelve weekly 1:1 sessions with Mitch (≈60 min each), prep + async messaging.
- Your AI strategy + governance framework: written, rehearsed in two of our sessions, presented to your actual board within the program window.
- One kickoff, one mid-program checkpoint, one program closing.
- Written program plan and the strategy's success criteria signed at kickoff.

**Outcome:**

> By week 12 you have stood in front of your board with an AI strategy and governance framework that is yours, that you can defend, and that maps to a sequenced transformation plan. You are no longer the executive who delegates the AI question; you are the executive who owns it.

**CTA:**

> `Book Command.`

**Bolt-on notes (below card):**

> Construct graduates can continue to Command for $60,000 (bolt-on rate, no second Foundation required).

## Detailed positioning notes (for site authoring + outreach reference; do not render verbatim on site)

### Who this is for

- 40+ exec running or about to run an enterprise AI transformation.
- Direct AI owner (CIO, CTO, CDO) at a regulated or large-enterprise business.
- CEO, COO, or business-unit head whose business strategy is now an AI strategy.
- Board director who is the AI lead on the board — needs the structure to ask the right questions of management.

### Who this is not for

- Anyone who wants personal fluency only (Catalyst).
- Anyone who wants to ship a tool (Construct).
- Anyone who is not yet at the strategic decision-making level — Command assumes the exec has the seat to act on what gets built in the program.

### Emotional acknowledgment

The Command buyer's worry is positional: *I am the person the board expects answers from on AI, and I do not yet have the framework I would defend in that room.* The program closes that gap by sending the exec INTO that room with the framework, having rehearsed it in two of the program sessions.

### Differentiator vs. generic AI strategy advisory

- Generic: a deck.
- Command: a framework the exec authored, rehearsed against a real audience (their own board), with a governance layer designed by an operator who has run JPMC- and Optus-scale AI programmes.

### Effective hourly economics (operator-side reference)

- Price: $100,000 list / $40,000 founding cohort.
- Mitch-hours: ~25 (same shape as Catalyst).
- Effective hourly (list): $4,000. Founding: ~$1,600.
- Bolt-on from Construct: $60,000 / 16 h = $3,750/h.

### Truth-gate

This track makes one claim worth flagging: that the exec presents their strategy "to your actual board" within the program window. This requires the exec to have a real board meeting falling within weeks 9–11. Operational reality: not every Command cohort will have this timing, so the SOW per-engagement notes whether the board presentation lands inside or just after the 12-week container. The site copy says "within the program window" because that is the default expectation; the SOW captures the actual.
