# Ribar.ai — Catalyst track

## Site card (verbatim)

**Eyebrow:**

> $25,000 · 12 weeks · 1:1

**Name:**

> Catalyst

**One-line promise:**

> *Personal AI activation. You become your own catalyst — daily fluency, daily practice, a personal AI workflow you actually use.*

**Inclusions:**

- 4-week shared Foundation: vocabulary, mental models, core tool fluency.
- 8-week Catalyst specialization: daily practice design, personal productivity, sustainable habit formation.
- Twelve weekly 1:1 sessions with Mitch (≈60 min each), prep + async messaging between sessions.
- A personal AI playbook you author across the program, ready to use after week 12.
- One kickoff, one mid-program checkpoint, one program closing.
- Written program plan and learning outcomes signed at kickoff.

**Outcome:**

> By week 12 you have a personal AI practice you run yourself — without needing to ask which tool to use, which prompt to write, or whether the output is good. You operate at the level of "I know what I want; I know how to get it."

**CTA:**

> `Book Catalyst.`

**Bolt-on note (below card):**

> Catalyst graduates can continue to Construct (12 weeks) for $30,000 — bolt-on rate, no second Foundation required.

## Detailed positioning notes (for site authoring + outreach reference; do not render verbatim on site)

### Who this is for

- 40+ exec who is comfortable with technology generally but feels the AI tools moving faster than their personal practice.
- Functional leader (CMO, CFO, CHRO) whose role is being reshaped by AI tools their team is using.
- Independent consultant or board director who needs personal AI fluency for credibility, not as a strategy capability.

### Who this is not for

- Anyone who needs to ship AI-built tools as part of their work (that is Construct).
- Anyone leading an AI transformation programme (that is Command).
- Anyone looking for generic "learn AI in 30 days" — Catalyst is one-on-one, paced to the exec, and outcome-led.

### Emotional acknowledgment

The Catalyst buyer's worry is internal: *I do not yet have the fluency I need to lead with confidence in AI conversations.* The program closes the gap quietly — by week 12 the exec runs their own practice and stops apologising for "still learning."

### Differentiator vs. generic AI upskilling

- Generic: cohort, recorded, "complete in your own time."
- Catalyst: 1:1, calendared, scoped to the exec's seat, with an operator on the other end who has shipped production AI.

### Effective hourly economics (operator-side reference)

- Price: $25,000 list / $10,000 founding cohort.
- Mitch-hours: ~25 (12 sessions @ 60 min + 6 h prep + 3 h kickoff/wrap + ~4 h async).
- Effective hourly (list): $1,000. Founding: ~$400. Both above the floor for premium B2C-flavoured 1:1 work.
