# Ribar.ai — Construct track

## Site card (verbatim)

**Eyebrow:**

> $50,000 · 12 weeks · 1:1

**Name:**

> Construct

**One-line promise:**

> *AI-powered building. You ship at least one real tool that solves a real problem in your work — built with AI as your second pair of hands.*

**Inclusions:**

- 4-week shared Foundation: vocabulary, mental models, core tool fluency (adapts deeper if you arrive already-fluent).
- 8-week Construct specialization: hands-on building with Claude Code or equivalent. Architecture, prompting, evaluation, deployment fundamentals.
- Twelve weekly 1:1 sessions with Mitch (≈60 min each), prep + async messaging.
- Your shipped tool — built across the program, in production by week 12.
- One kickoff, one mid-program checkpoint, one program closing.
- Written program plan and the tool's success criteria signed at kickoff.

**Outcome:**

> By week 12 you have shipped at least one AI-built tool that solves a real problem in your work. You can read the code, you can iterate on it, you can defend the architecture to your team. You are no longer dependent on a vendor to test an AI hypothesis.

**CTA:**

> `Book Construct.`

**Bolt-on notes (below card):**

> Catalyst graduates can continue to Construct for $30,000 (bolt-on rate, no second Foundation required). Construct graduates can continue to Command for $60,000.

## Detailed positioning notes (for site authoring + outreach reference; do not render verbatim on site)

### Who this is for

- 40+ exec who already has Catalyst-level fluency (whether self-taught or via Catalyst track) and wants to build, not just operate.
- Direct AI owner (CIO, CTO, CDO) who needs to make architecture decisions and wants the hands-on instinct to make them well.
- Functional leader who has identified a specific problem AI could solve in their domain and wants to ship a working tool.
- Startup founder building an AI-native product who wants senior 1:1 guidance on architecture and shipping.

### Who this is not for

- Anyone who wants daily personal AI fluency only — Construct demands shipping (Catalyst is the right fit).
- Anyone looking for strategic AI leadership advice without the build component (Command).
- Anyone who wants to learn coding from scratch — Construct assumes the exec can think systematically and follow technical conversations.

### Emotional acknowledgment

The Construct buyer's worry is operational: *I keep being told what AI can do; I have not yet built anything with it.* The program closes that gap with a tangible shipped artifact, not a certificate.

### Differentiator vs. generic AI builder programs

- Generic: tutorials, sample apps, "build a chatbot."
- Construct: scoped to a real problem in the exec's work, shipped to production, defensible to their engineering team.

### Effective hourly economics (operator-side reference)

- Price: $50,000 list / $20,000 founding cohort.
- Mitch-hours: ~25 (same shape as Catalyst).
- Effective hourly (list): $2,000. Founding: ~$800.
- Bolt-on from Catalyst: $30,000 / 16 h = $1,875/h.
