# Ribar.ai — hero and positioning copy

Positioning is layered per the brainstorm:

| Layer | Function | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Top | Operator + Credential | MIT + JPMC + Optus anchor |
| Middle | Emotional | "I'm 40+, I've navigated this, I'll guide you. The 'left-behind' fear is real; here's the way through." |
| Bottom | Structure | Three tracks revealed at the tracks section, not at the headline |

Hero copy ladders these in the order: H1 = Operator + Credential, lede = Emotional, CTAs route to Structure.

## Primary recommendation (ship this)

**Eyebrow:**

> For senior leaders activating with AI.

**H1:**

> MIT-trained operator. Built production AI at JPMorgan Chase and led retention recovery at Optus. Now coaching senior leaders through the AI shift.

**Lede:**

> If you are forty-plus and watching AI reshape your role in real time — or sensing your seat at the table being reshaped without your say in it — you are in the right place. I have navigated this shift inside two of the largest AI programmes in banking and telco. I will help you activate.

**CTAs:**

- Primary: `See the three tracks.`
- Secondary: `Talk to Mitch.`

**Operator credential micro-block (beneath the hero, before the tracks):**

> This program is run by an operator. Not a coach with a deck. Not a vendor pitching a tool. I have owned P&Ls, run AI programmes, and shipped systems that are still in production. The 1:1 sessions you will sit in with me are the same conversations I have with peers — adapted to the seat you sit in.

## Ogilvy four-test review of the primary recommendation

| Test | Pass / Fail | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Specific | Pass | MIT, JPMorgan Chase, Optus, "twelve-week" are concrete. |
| Believable | Pass | Bio anchors are verifiable in principle; the "you are in the right place" framing is honest invitation, not a claim. |
| Important | Pass | "Watching AI reshape your role" is the exact frame the 40+ buyer carries. |
| Exclusive | Pass | The combination of MIT + JPMC + Optus + 1:1 is not replicable by a generic coach or vendor program. |

## What the hero must NOT do

- Use "AI shift" without acknowledging the buyer's emotional state. Generic AI coaching pages do this; that is the noise the buyer is trying to filter out.
- Lead with the price. Price reveals at the tracks section.
- Use generic "coach" or "facilitator" language. The operator anchor is the differentiator.
- Use AI-cliché imagery (neural nets, robot hands, glowing brains) in any accompanying visual.

## Voice notes (carry sitewide)

- First-person ("I will help you activate"), not third-person ("Mitch will help you").
- Direct, never patronising. The reader is more senior than the writer in most domains; the writer's authority is on the AI activation problem specifically.
- Concrete verbs ("build", "ship", "lead", "navigate"), not consultant verbs ("enable", "empower", "transform").
- Acknowledge the emotional state once per page; do not repeat.
