# Ribar.ai — FAQ

Eight questions, buyer-objection-shaped per Ogilvy direction inherited from Capita. Sits between Founder Letter and Contact.

---

## Section H2

> What execs ask me before they sign.

---

## Q1 — Who is this actually for?

**Q.** Who is this actually for?

**A.** Forty-plus senior leaders who are navigating an AI shift in their role and want one-to-one work with an operator who has done the same thing inside a large business. The buyer is usually a CMO, CFO, CHRO, COO, CIO, CTO, CDO, business-unit head, board director, or independent consultant who is being asked AI questions they cannot yet answer the way they want to. The buyer is not someone looking for a recorded course or a cohort program.

## Q2 — What if I am a complete beginner with AI?

**Q.** What if I am a complete beginner with AI, or what if I am already deep in Claude Code?

**A.** Either way works. The 4-week Foundation adapts to where you arrive. A beginner gets a slower, more vocabulary-heavy start; a Claude Code user goes immediately deeper on edge cases and architecture. The 1:1 format is what makes this possible — every session is scoped to you, not to a cohort average.

## Q3 — What does the price cover?

**Q.** What does the price cover, and is there anything additional I should expect?

**A.** The price covers twelve weeks of 1:1 sessions with me, prep work I do before each session, async messaging between sessions, and the written program plan signed at kickoff. There are no extras — no per-session add-ons, no platform fees, no software licenses you have to buy separately. If you choose Catalyst, the price is $25,000. Construct is $50,000. Command is $100,000. Bolt-ons from one track to the next are $30,000 (Catalyst → Construct) and $60,000 (Construct → Command).

## Q4 — How is this different from a generic AI coaching program?

**Q.** How is this different from a generic AI coaching program?

**A.** Generic AI coaching is usually delivered by people whose primary credential is that they teach AI coaching. I am an operator. I built production AI at JPMorgan Chase. I ran retention recovery at Optus. I owned a P&L at Brand Networks. The sessions you sit in with me are the same conversations I have with peers, adapted to the seat you sit in. The differentiator is not technique — it is who is on the other end of the email.

## Q5 — What if I want to start later, or pause part-way through?

**Q.** What if I want to start later, or pause part-way through?

**A.** The 12-week container is fixed; the start date is flexible. You can defer a start by up to 60 days from signing without re-pricing. Mid-program pauses are accommodated for unavoidable reasons (medical, bereavement, major business event) — the SOW captures the terms. Voluntary pauses for convenience are not the model; the cadence is what makes this work.

## Q6 — Do you do group programs or a cohort version?

**Q.** Do you do group programs or a cohort version?

**A.** No. 1:1 only. A cohort version would change the product fundamentally. The opt-in community for graduates will fire when client volume justifies it; until then, every interaction is one-to-one.

## Q7 — What happens after the program ends?

**Q.** What happens after the program ends?

**A.** Three options. (1) You step out, take what you built, and run with it. (2) You continue with optional ongoing support on a monthly retainer (set at signing or after). (3) You graduate into the next track via the bolt-on rate: Catalyst → Construct for $30,000, Construct → Command for $60,000. The bolt-ons skip the second Foundation, so you go straight to the new specialization.

## Q8 — How do I know this will work for me?

**Q.** How do I know this will work for me?

**A.** Three things. First, the discovery call is free, 30 minutes, and I will tell you if a track does not fit. Second, the program plan signed at kickoff names the indicators we expect to move; if those indicators do not move at the 90-day mark, I return for a no-fee follow-up week. Third, every program is principal-led — there is no analyst layer, no second-tier coach, no curriculum platform between you and me. The risk you are buying down is the risk of an operator misreading your situation, and the discovery call exists to surface that before either of us commits.
