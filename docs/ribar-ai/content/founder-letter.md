## Verbatim copy

**Section label:** From Mitch.

**H2:** Why I built this.

---

I am Mitch Ribar. I have spent the last twenty years inside the leadership teams of large, regulated businesses — telco, banking, financial services, SaaS — leading retention recovery at Optus, running the AI Personalization programme at JPMorgan Chase, owning the P&L at Brand Networks, and building financial-services products at Quantium. I have an MBA from MIT Sloan. Most of what I have done, I have done in rooms where the cost of getting AI wrong was not academic.

I built Ribar.ai for one reason. Senior leaders my age — forty, fifty, sixty — are watching AI reshape their roles in real time, and most of what is on offer to them is either generic upskilling or vendor sales motion dressed up as education. The first is patronising. The second is a conflict of interest. Neither acknowledges that the people most affected by this shift are the ones who built the systems AI is now layering over.

If you are senior, you have already learned multiple platforms across your career. You watched the web replace direct mail. You watched mobile replace web. You watched cloud replace on-premise. You know how this works. You also know that none of those shifts were navigated by sitting through a recorded course at 1.5× speed. They were navigated by doing the work, with someone who had already done it, in a structure that did not waste your time.

That is what this is. Twelve weeks. One-to-one. Three tracks, depending on where you want to land:

- **Catalyst** if you want a personal AI practice you actually run.
- **Construct** if you want to ship at least one real AI-built tool that solves a real problem in your work.
- **Command** if you want an AI strategy you have presented — rehearsed in this program — to your actual board.

Every track is principal-led. By me. There is no junior coach, no curriculum platform you log into between sessions, no certificate. There is a working session each week, prep before, async between, and at the end of twelve weeks you have something you did not have when you started.

The price is published on this page. If you are a founding-cohort client (and you will know if you are, because I have invited you personally), the price is different and the contract carries a testimonial clause. If you are not, the published price is what you pay.

There is a 90-day commitment. If, at the 90-day mark after your program closes, the indicators we defined together have not moved, I return for a no-fee follow-up week to find out why. That commitment is in every statement of work.

If this fits, the next move is a 30-minute conversation. I respond within two business days.

— *Mitchell Ribar.*
