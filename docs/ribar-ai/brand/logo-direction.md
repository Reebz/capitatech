# Ribar.ai — logo direction (v1 brief)

This is a brief for a future logo design engagement, not a finished logo. The brief captures direction, references, and what NOT to do so the eventual designer has a tight specification to work from.

---

## Brand essence (one paragraph)

Ribar.ai is a 12-week 1:1 AI executive program led by an MIT-trained operator who has built production AI at JPMorgan Chase and led retention recovery at Optus. The buyer is a forty-plus senior leader navigating the AI shift in their role. The brand register is: operator-led, personal, premium, considered, not Silicon Valley.

---

## What the logo must communicate

1. **Personal brand.** This is Mitch's program, not a faceless edtech platform.
2. **Operator credibility.** Reads as built-by-someone-who-has-shipped, not built-by-a-marketer.
3. **AI domain WITHOUT AI cliché.** The buyer knows the field; do not pander.
4. **Premium register at price points $25k–$100k.** Not low-cost, not mass-market.

---

## Form direction (options to brief the designer with)

### Option A — Wordmark only

`Ribar.ai` rendered in a custom-tightened Geist Variable at 900 weight, with the `.ai` styled as a subtle de-emphasised suffix in the Signal Amber accent colour. The wordmark IS the logo; no glyph.

**Pros:** ships fastest, cheapest, future-proof (works in any size). Matches personal-brand register.
**Cons:** no glyph means no usable favicon-style shorthand at small sizes.

**Likely best fit for v1.** Ship this; commission glyph at v2 if needed.

### Option B — Wordmark + minimalist mark

Wordmark per Option A, plus a glyph that pairs alongside. Glyph options:

- A single bold `R` in the Operator Indigo, used as favicon and avatar.
- A geometric "framework" mark (no AI imagery — a rotated square, a single bold dot, a structural element that reads as "operator architecture").

### Option C — Signature mark (Mitch's signature as logomark)

Use Mitch's actual signature (the SVG already produced for the Capita founder letter) as the brand mark. Pair with the wordmark beneath.

**Pros:** strongest personal-brand signal possible. Ogilvy-aligned (Schweppes Commander Whitehead, Hathaway eye-patch). Already-extant asset; zero new commission.
**Cons:** signature may not scale well at tiny sizes (favicon); requires the signature to be visually clean.

**Worth testing for v1 — if the signature asset is clean, Option C is the highest-leverage choice.**

---

## Colour usage in the logo

- Primary: Operator Indigo (`#1E1B4B` from `identity-v1.md`).
- Accent (use sparingly — only the `.ai` suffix or a single mark element): Signal Amber (`#F59E0B`).
- Reverse: pure white on dark surfaces (footer, dark sections).
- Monochrome: pure black version available for embossing, print, and grayscale contexts.

The logo must work in:

- Full colour
- Reverse (white on dark)
- Pure monochrome (black on white)
- Single colour (when print constraints require)

---

## Format deliverables (when designer commissions)

- `logo-wordmark.svg` — primary lockup, scalable.
- `logo-mark.svg` — glyph only (if Option B/C).
- `logo-wordmark-reverse.svg` — white version.
- `logo-mark-favicon-32.png`, `-180.png` — favicon raster fallbacks at the same sizes Capita uses.
- `og-image-1200x630.png` — social card image with logo + tagline.
- `apple-touch-icon.png` — 180×180.
- `manifest.json` — PWA manifest (mirroring Capita's pattern).

---

## What the logo must NOT do

- No neural-network imagery.
- No mesh / data-stream / abstract-AI motif.
- No robot iconography (no eyes, no hands, no faces).
- No brain imagery.
- No swooshes, no infinity loops, no "innovation" gradients.
- No emojis or emoji-style icons.
- No serifed wordmark (does not match Geist register).
- No lowercase-only "ribar.ai" (loses the personal-brand weight).
- No tagline embedded in the logo (taglines belong in copy, not in lockups).

---

## Inspirational references (study, do not copy)

- **Linear** (linear.app) — wordmark + minimal mark, premium register.
- **Vercel** — wordmark + black-triangle mark.
- **Mercury** — wordmark with mathematical proportions.
- **Stripe** — wordmark with subtle gradient accent.
- **Pirsig's "Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance" book covers** — for the operator-considered register, not for the form.
- **Hathaway eye-patch ads** (Ogilvy) — for the signature-as-mark direction.

DO NOT reference: any "AI startup landing page" generic Dribbble shots, any "neural" or "intelligence" brand systems, any Web3/crypto branding.

I need a v1 brand mark that communicates personal brand + operator credibility at a premium register, without any AI imagery (neural nets, robots, brains). Palette: Operator Indigo `#1E1B4B` + Signal Amber `#F59E0B`. References to study (not copy): Linear, Vercel, Mercury, Stripe.
