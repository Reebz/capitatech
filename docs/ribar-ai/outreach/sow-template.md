# Ribar.ai — Statement of Work template (founding cohort)

Template for the founding-cohort SOW. Contains the testimonial clause that is the contractual exchange for the founding price. NOT legal advice — must be reviewed by Mitch's solicitor before use. The legal terms below are placeholders to capture intent; the lawyer-reviewed version supersedes.

Founding-cohort price applies only when (a) the client is on the explicit founding-cohort target list AND (b) one of the first ten signed clients OR signs before end of Q3 2026, whichever comes first. After conditions lapse, Ladder A pricing applies.

---

## SOW preamble

> **Statement of Work — Ribar.ai [Track Name]**
>
> **Reference:** SOW-RIBAR-[YYYY-MM-NN]
> **Effective date:** [Date]
> **Client:** [Legal entity name]
> **Client representative:** [Name, title]
> **Service provider:** Ribar.ai (a trading name of [Mitch Ribar's legal entity])
> **Service provider representative:** Mitchell Ribar, Principal
>
> This Statement of Work ("SOW") sets out the scope, schedule, fees, and other terms under which the Service Provider will deliver the Ribar.ai [Track Name] programme to the Client. The SOW is subject to the Master Services Agreement (MSA) executed between the parties on [Date]. In the event of conflict between this SOW and the MSA, the MSA prevails except where this SOW expressly states otherwise.

---

## 1. Scope of work

> **1.1 Programme.** The Service Provider will deliver the Ribar.ai [Catalyst / Construct / Command] programme over twelve (12) weeks, commencing [Start date]. The programme consists of (i) a four-week Foundation phase and (ii) an eight-week [Track Name] Specialization phase, delivered as 1:1 sessions between the Service Provider Representative and the Client Representative.
>
> **1.2 Sessions.** Twelve (12) weekly 1:1 sessions of approximately sixty (60) minutes each, scheduled in advance via the Service Provider's calendar system. Async messaging between sessions is included and capped at reasonable use; no specific volume commitment is made.
>
> **1.3 Deliverables.** As set out in Annex A — Programme deliverables. Deliverables vary by track and are finalised at programme kickoff.
>
> **1.4 Exclusions.** Anything not expressly listed in 1.1–1.3 or in Annex A is excluded, including (but not limited to): group sessions, additional individuals beyond the Client Representative, custom software development outside Construct track deliverables, hardware procurement, third-party software licences, and travel.

---

## 2. Schedule

> **2.1 Commencement.** [Start date].
> **2.2 Completion.** Twelve (12) weeks after Commencement, on or about [End date].
> **2.3 Cadence.** Weekly 1:1 sessions, day and time set at kickoff.
> **2.4 Deferral.** The Client may defer Commencement by up to sixty (60) days from the date of SOW execution without re-pricing.
> **2.5 Pause.** Mid-programme pauses are accommodated for unavoidable reasons (medical, bereavement, major business event) on written notice. Voluntary pauses for convenience are not contemplated.

---

## 3. Fees

> **3.1 Fee.** AUD $[founding-cohort fee, per track: $10k Catalyst / $20k Construct / $40k Command], exclusive of GST.
> **3.2 Founding-cohort designation.** This fee is offered under the Service Provider's founding-cohort programme. It is contingent on (a) the Client being on the Service Provider's explicit founding-cohort target list and (b) this SOW being executed before the founding-cohort cap is reached (the first ten clients OR end of Q3 2026, whichever comes first). The Client acknowledges that the founding-cohort fee is not a published discount and that the Service Provider's standard published fees (Ladder A) are: Catalyst AUD $25,000; Construct AUD $50,000; Command AUD $100,000.
> **3.3 Payment terms.** Invoice issued on SOW execution, payable net fourteen (14) days. Programme commencement scheduled after payment receipt.
> **3.4 Currency.** Australian dollars. Conversion at then-current rate by the Client's payment provider if paying in a foreign currency.

---

## 4. Testimonial clause

> **4.1 Testimonial obligation.** As a material condition of the founding-cohort fee, the Client agrees to provide a written, named testimonial to the Service Provider within sixty (60) days following the conclusion of the programme.
>
> **4.2 Testimonial scope.** The testimonial shall:
>
> - Be authored by the Client Representative or another senior officer of the Client mutually agreed in advance;
> - Be attributable to the author's full name, title, and the Client's legal name (or, with Service Provider's agreement, an equivalent named industry-and-title attribution);
> - Be of a length and substantive quality commensurate with similar executive testimonials in the senior advisory market (~80–250 words);
> - Describe (without breaching confidentiality) the outcomes of the programme.
>
> **4.3 Right of use.** The Client grants the Service Provider a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to reproduce, distribute, and display the testimonial on the Service Provider's website (`ribar.ai`), in outreach material, and in any marketing material related to the Ribar.ai programme.
>
> **4.4 Confidentiality of the testimonial drafting process.** The Client may submit drafts for the Client's review and approval, and the Service Provider will not publish a testimonial that the Client has not approved in writing. However, the Client agrees not to unreasonably withhold approval of testimonials that fairly describe the programme.
>
> **4.5 Failure to provide.** If the Client fails to provide a testimonial within sixty (60) days following programme conclusion (and after written reminder by the Service Provider), the Client agrees to pay the difference between the founding-cohort fee and the then-current Ladder A published fee for the relevant track, within thirty (30) days. This is the agreed remedy for breach of this clause and is not a penalty.

---

## 5. 90-day commitment

> **5.1 Indicators.** At programme kickoff, the parties shall jointly define the indicators by which the programme's effectiveness will be measured at the 90-day post-completion mark. The indicators shall be set out in Annex B — 90-day indicators.
>
> **5.2 Check-in.** A 90-day post-completion check-in is included in the fee. At or around the 90-day mark, the parties shall jointly review the indicators.
>
> **5.3 No-fee follow-up week.** If the agreed indicators have not moved at the 90-day check-in, the Service Provider shall return for a follow-up week (up to four (4) additional 1:1 sessions plus reasonable async time) at no additional fee, to identify causes and recommend remedies.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

> **6.1 Mutual NDA.** Both parties shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed under this SOW. Mutual NDA terms apply per the MSA.
> **6.2 Programme content.** The Client may freely use, internally, all materials, frameworks, and outputs created during the programme. The Client may not re-distribute the Service Provider's proprietary programme curriculum to third parties.
> **6.3 Testimonial exception.** The testimonial under clause 4 is the only Client-produced material the Service Provider may publish externally; all other Client material remains confidential.

---

## 7. Intellectual property

> **7.1 Pre-existing IP.** Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing IP.
> **7.2 Programme output.** Work product created during the programme is jointly developed; the Client owns the IP in any artifacts (decks, models, code) created for the Client's use. The Service Provider retains the right to use sanitised, anonymised learnings to improve the programme curriculum.
> **7.3 Construct track exception.** Code shipped as part of the Construct track is owned outright by the Client.

---

## 8. Termination

> **8.1 For cause.** Either party may terminate for material uncured breach.
> **8.2 For convenience by the Client.** The Client may terminate for convenience on fourteen (14) days' written notice, subject to payment for sessions delivered to date (pro-rated against the twelve-session schedule), plus a wind-down fee of [10–20%] of the unpaid fee balance.
> **8.3 For convenience by the Service Provider.** The Service Provider may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice, subject to refund of any unearned fee.

---

## 9. Governing law

> **9.1 Jurisdiction.** This SOW is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia, and the parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of that jurisdiction.

## Annex B — 90-day indicators

To be drafted at kickoff. Sample indicators per track:

- **Catalyst:** the personal AI playbook is still in active daily use; three or more rituals are operating; the exec can name a non-trivial decision made with AI-assisted prep in the prior 30 days.
- **Construct:** the shipped tool is still in production; at least one other user has used it in the prior 30 days; total cost-of-ownership is within initial projection.
- **Command:** the AI strategy presented to the board is either in execution or has been revised based on board feedback; the governance framework is operating; the talent plan is on track.
