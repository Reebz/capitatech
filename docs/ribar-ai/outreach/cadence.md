# Ribar.ai — founding-cohort outreach cadence + channel plan

The cadence has to be sustainable for Mitch as a solo operator. Spray-and-pray burns reputation; under-volume burns the Q3 2026 deadline. The plan below targets 5–10 personalised outreaches per week with structured follow-ups.

---

## Weekly cadence (sustainable single-operator baseline)

| Day | Activity | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Wave planning. Review the Notion target list, decide that week's 5–10 outreaches, decide which template fits each. | 30 min |
| **Tuesday** | Outreach send (LinkedIn DM + email). Each message is personalised — no batch send. | 60–90 min |
| **Wednesday** | Follow-ups on prior-wave outreaches (per the follow-up schedule below). | 30–60 min |
| **Thursday** | Discovery calls (scheduled in advance via Cal.com). | Up to 3 hours |
| **Friday** | Notion update. Mark statuses, capture notes from week's discovery calls, plan next week's targets. | 30 min |

Total: ~6–10 hours of outreach work per week. Sustainable alongside delivering active engagements.

---

## Follow-up schedule

| If touch | Then next touch |
|---|---|
| **Initial outreach** | Day 0 |
| **Soft bump** (LinkedIn or email) | Day 7 if no reply |
| **Final follow-up** | Day 21 if no reply |
| **Dormant** | Move to Notion `Status = Declined` after day 21 final follow-up with no reply. Re-evaluate in 6 months for re-engagement opportunity. |

Three touches max per outreach cycle. No more — the asymmetry of effort vs. yield collapses, and additional touches feel pushy.

## Channel selection

### LinkedIn DM (primary channel)

- **Use for:** 1st-degree connections, mutual-connection introductions, MIT Sloan alumni, conference contacts.
- **Voice:** First-person, conversational, length under 200 words.
- **Hook:** Acknowledge a specific shared context — recent post, role change, mutual connection.
- **CTA:** Soft — "if this resonates, happy to send the page" or "30-minute conversation if you're curious."
- **Template:** `docs/ribar-ai/outreach/messages/intro-cold.md` (for cold within 1st-degree) or `intro-warm.md` (for someone Mitch has worked with).

### Email (secondary channel)

- **Use for:** Past colleagues whose LinkedIn is inactive; conference introductions where business cards were exchanged; referrals from existing clients.
- **Voice:** First-person, business-formal but not corporate, length under 250 words.
- **Hook:** Explicitly name the channel that introduced you ("Sarah Lin suggested I reach out").
- **CTA:** Direct — "would a 30-minute conversation in the next two weeks work?"
- **Template:** Adapt LinkedIn templates for email format; add subject line.

### In-person at events (high-conversion, low-volume channel)

- **Use for:** Conferences, alumni meetups, industry roundtables where Mitch is present.
- **Voice:** Conversational. No pitch; explore the AI activation problem first.
- **Hook:** "What is the AI question that keeps coming back to you at the moment?"
- **CTA:** Exchange contacts. Follow up within 48 hours via the relevant channel.

### Inbound (when site is live)

- **Use for:** Anyone who arrives via the contact form or Cal.com booking.
- **Voice:** Acknowledgement first, scoping conversation second.
- **Hook:** Reference what they wrote in the form.
- **CTA:** Discovery call booked within five business days.

### Referral from existing client (highest-quality channel)

- **Use for:** Anyone introduced by an existing Capita Advisory client or a past Ribar.ai cohort member.
- **Voice:** First-person, lightly informal, acknowledging the introducer.
- **Hook:** "[Introducer's name] suggested we connect — they mentioned [specific context]."
- **CTA:** Direct discovery-call invitation.
- **Template:** Adapt warm intro template; explicitly thank the introducer in the close.

## What to NOT do

- **No mass-send tooling.** Every outreach is hand-written by Mitch. Outreach automation would land Ribar.ai in the same noise the buyer is trying to escape.
- **No paid InMail blasts.** Same reason.
- **No "free coaching call" framing.** The discovery call is a fit conversation, not a sales call. Calling it "free coaching" cheapens the program.
- **No mentioning the founding-cohort discount in the cold outreach.** The discount surfaces only after qualifying. Leading with it primes the wrong selection: people optimising for discount, not for fit.
- **No social posts about the founding-cohort discount.** Per plan KD7 — never published.
- **No bulk LinkedIn connection requests with "I'll send a program brief" follow-up.** This is the precise behavior the target audience filters out.

## Tracking and review

- All outreaches logged in Notion `Ribar.ai Founding Targets` database (see `target-list-criteria.md`).
- Weekly Friday review: how many sent, how many replied, how many discovery calls booked, how many signed.
- Quarterly review: are the assumptions (20% reply rate, 33% close from discovery) holding? If not, recalibrate volume targets.

---

## Discontinuation criteria

Pause founding-cohort outreach when ANY of the following:

- 10 founding-cohort clients signed.
- End of Q3 2026 (calendar date: 2026-09-30).
- Mitch's calendar capacity is at the 6-concurrent-engagement ceiling for the next two months.

After discontinuation: outreach shifts to Ladder A pricing only; the founding-cohort template is archived; the SOW template is updated to remove the testimonial clause and price discount.
