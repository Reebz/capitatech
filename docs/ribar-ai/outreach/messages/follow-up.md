# Ribar.ai outreach — follow-up template

Two follow-ups per outreach cycle. Soft bump at day 7. Final at day 21. After that, the lead moves to `Declined` in Notion and is re-evaluated in 6 months.

---

## Follow-up 1 — soft bump (Day 7)

Brief. Reference the original message. Add ONE piece of new value (a recent article you read that maps to their world, a small reframe, a question). Do NOT just say "bumping this."

### LinkedIn DM version

> [First name],
>
> Bumping the note from last week in case it slid past — [one-sentence value-add: a relevant link, a question you genuinely want their answer to, a small reframe of why this might land for them].
>
> If a 30-minute conversation works in the next couple of weeks, the calendar is here: [Cal.com link]. If not, no follow-up beyond this — I will leave the door open for later.
>
> — Mitch

### Examples of the "one piece of new value"

- *"I read [specific article on AI activation in your sector] this week and thought of the conversation I was hoping to have with you."*
- *"One question I am asking everyone in the founding cohort target list: what is the AI activation question that has been on your mind longest without resolution? Even a one-line reply is useful."*
- *"A small reframe — Ribar.ai is not 'learn AI.' It is 'navigate the AI activation question on your own desk, with an operator.' Different shape."*

### What NOT to do in the soft bump

- Do NOT just say "did you see my message" or "bumping this." Reads as cold-outreach automation.
- Do NOT add pressure ("the founding cohort is filling up"). Per plan KD7, do not weaponise scarcity in outreach.
- Do NOT switch channels mid-cycle (LinkedIn → email) unless the original channel is verifiably blocked. Channel-switching during a cold-outreach cycle reads as desperate.

---

## Follow-up 2 — final (Day 21)

Honest, brief, low-pressure. Acknowledges that the timing may not be right. Leaves the door open for later. This is the message that often gets a reply months later when the timing IS right.

### LinkedIn DM version

> [First name],
>
> Last note from me on this. The founding cohort is open for [time-bound — "the next few months" or "until end of Q3"], and I have got the calendar held for the right conversations. If now is not the right time, no problem at all.
>
> If you ever want to revisit — even a year from now — drop me a line.
>
> Site at [ribar.ai] for whenever.
>
> — Mitch

### What this template does

- Names that the founding-cohort window is finite without manipulating scarcity.
- Tells the recipient "no problem at all" — removes any pressure tax on the relationship.
- Explicitly invites a future reply, which is when many of these conversations actually start.
- Closes professionally so the relationship is preserved.

### What NOT to do in the final follow-up

- Do NOT guilt-trip ("I have not heard back from you despite reaching out twice").
- Do NOT chase further. Three touches max. Anything beyond reads as harassment.
- Do NOT offer a discount or a special concession at this stage. The cohort terms are the cohort terms.

---

## After the final follow-up

If no reply by day 28 (final follow-up + 1 week):

- Move Notion `Status` to `Declined` with `Notes = "no reply after 3-touch cycle [date]"`.
- Re-evaluate in 6 months. If their role has changed, their company has changed, or you have new mutual context, the next outreach is a fresh cold or warm — NOT a continuation of the old thread.

If the recipient eventually replies (days later, weeks later, months later):

- Respond normally. The lapsed thread is not a sign of disinterest; senior leaders' inboxes are noisy. Treat the reply as the first real conversation.

---

## When NOT to follow up

- The recipient explicitly said "not interested" in their reply to the original outreach. Move to `Declined` immediately; no follow-up.
- The recipient signalled disinterest indirectly (very brief reply, polite-deflection language). Read the signal; do not push.
- The recipient is in a public reputational crisis (legal, regulatory, business). Wait until the situation clarifies, then re-evaluate whether they are still a fit per `target-list-criteria.md`.
