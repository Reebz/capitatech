# Ribar.ai outreach — cold intro template

Length target: under 200 words. First-person. Personalised with [bracketed] fields that must be filled in per recipient — never send without personalisation.

The cold template is sent to people Mitch has not previously worked with but who share recognisable context (mutual connection, alumni network, industry role). The goal is to open a 30-minute conversation, not to pitch the program.

---

## LinkedIn DM version

**Subject (if email; LinkedIn DMs are subjectless):** A 30-minute conversation on AI activation for senior leaders

---

> [First name],
>
> [Specific opener — mutual connection, recent post they wrote, role change, alumni context. Two sentences max. Examples:
> — "I noticed your move into the [new role] at [company] — congratulations, and I imagine the AI portfolio just landed on your desk."
> — "We are both Sloan alums (I was [year]); I have been watching your work at [company] for a while."
> — "[Mutual name] mentioned your work on [specific thing] and I thought I would reach out directly." ]

> Quick context on me: I spent twenty years inside leadership teams at Optus (retention recovery), JPMorgan Chase (AI Personalization programme), and Brand Networks (P&L). MIT Sloan MBA. I am now running a 1:1 AI executive program for forty-plus senior leaders — three tracks (Catalyst for personal activation, Construct for shipping AI-built tools, Command for strategic AI leadership), twelve weeks each.
>

If you are navigating an AI activation question of your own — personal practice, building something with AI, or the strategy you are about to present to your board — I would value a 30-minute conversation to see whether one of the tracks fits.
>
> The site (still rolling out) is at [ribar.ai or coming-soon link]. If a call works in the next two weeks, here is the calendar: [Cal.com link].
>

---

## Notes on personalisation

- The first sentence MUST reference something specific to the recipient. If you cannot find one, do not send.
- "Alumni context" only applies if the alumni connection is genuine and recent enough to be relevant.
- The "[mutual connection]" line only works if you have the mutual's permission to name them. Always ask first; never namedrop without consent.

## Notes on what NOT to include

- Do NOT mention the founding-cohort discount in this message. The discount surfaces only AFTER the discovery call when fit is confirmed. Leading with discount primes the wrong audience selection.
- Do NOT call this "coaching" or "training." It is a program; it is 1:1; it produces a deliverable. Mislabelling lowers the register.
- Do NOT attach a brochure, deck, or PDF. The site URL is the long-form. Attachments in cold outreach get caught by spam filters and read as low-effort.
- Do NOT use the phrase "I would love to" — common LinkedIn boilerplate; reads as templated.

## Reply paths

When the recipient replies:

- **Positive ("yes, let's talk"):** confirm the Cal.com booking; acknowledge their reply in one sentence. Do NOT send program brief in advance — the discovery call is the qualifying conversation.
- **Asking for more info:** point to the site (`ribar.ai`) and the Cal.com link. Do NOT respond with a long-form pitch in email — the site IS the long-form.
- **Declines politely:** acknowledge with one line, thank them, leave the door open. Move Notion status to `Declined`. Re-evaluate in 6 months.
- **No reply:** schedule follow-up per cadence (`docs/ribar-ai/outreach/cadence.md`).
